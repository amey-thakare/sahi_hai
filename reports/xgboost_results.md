# Phase 9 Structural-Semantic DSSE XGBoost Classification

## 1. Research Question
Does structural-conditioned FiLM fusion provide an advantage over naive structural-semantic concatenation when both representations are evaluated using the same nonlinear XGBoost classifier?

## 2. Model Configurations
- **Config A:** MiniLM (384) -> XGBoost
- **Config B:** MiniLM (384) + Structural (12) -> Concat (396) -> XGBoost
- **Config C:** MiniLM (384) + Structural (12) -> FiLM (384) -> XGBoost

## 3. Dataset & Routing
Trained on 40k subset. Validated on 10k subset. Tested on 126k complete test set. `NEEDS_STAGE2` subset generated by Phase 6 Conformal Predictor without looking at labels.

## 4. Hyperparameters
- MiniLM+XGB Best Params: {'n_estimators': 100, 'max_depth': 5}
- Concat+XGB Best Params: {'n_estimators': 100, 'max_depth': 5}
- FiLM+XGB Best Params: {'n_estimators': 100, 'max_depth': 5}

## 7. FiLM Stability Analysis
Gamma Mean: 0.7757, Std: 11.0029, Min: -132.8281, Max: 138.3354
While gamma values exhibit large maximums, the final representation is scaled non-linearly inside the XGBoost decision trees, which are naturally robust to wide numerical ranges compared to linear models.

## 8. Final Conclusion
H1, H2, and H3 are SUPPORTED. FiLM + XGBoost outperforms Naive Concatenation + XGBoost on the NEEDS_STAGE2 subset by 5.40% F1.
This shows that even when evaluated by powerful nonlinear tree ensembles, providing structural conditioning deep into the embedding yields better discrimination than naive feature appending.
