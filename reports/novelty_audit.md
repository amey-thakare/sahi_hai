# Novelty Audit

The paper has been thoroughly audited for unsupported novelty claims (e.g., "novel", "first", "new", "unprecedented", "state-of-the-art", "superior").

## Findings:
- No claims of "state-of-the-art" are present.
- No claims of "first to..." are present.
- The paper correctly uses precise claims such as "We propose...", "The primary contribution...".
- In the Ablation Study, it states FiLM "achieved a higher observed F1 score", avoiding unsubstantiated statistical superiority claims since it was a historical subset evaluation.
- McNemar's test correctly claims an asymmetry in paired differences, not superiority.

## Conclusion:
The paper avoids hyperbolic novelty claims and accurately describes its contributions without exaggerating the scope of its originality.
